about me

Hi, I’m Mina. I’m so glad you’ve found me.

I’m a social worker, postpartum doula, and mother of three. I’m also a wife, a daughter, a sister, a friend, and a woman embracing the power of her femininity. My whole life I have been called to help and heal others, particularly those going through vulnerable times.

I am a natural nurturer, with an array of holistic approaches to share for personal and interpersonal healing and growth based on what is going on within you and in your life. I draw my approach from my education and experience as a social worker and postpartum doula, my own three very different postpartum experiences, my motherhood journey as it continues to unfold, my 20+ years of yoga and meditation practice, experience with somatic and sound healing, along with my deep connection to nature and spirit and the universe.

If you are struggling or seeking support in pregnancy, postpartum or in any early stage of motherhood, please know you are not alone. I come from a place of deep compassion, non-judgement, and pure intention to support you and your family in a way that works for you.
